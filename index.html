Introduction

Recommender systems (RecSys) play a pivotal role in helping users navigate, discover, and consume massive and ever-changing information. Yet most deployed systems still rely on static user profiles and pre-computed recommendation actions that fail to adapt within or across sessions. As personalization demands intensify, privacy regulations tighten, and user intent grows more dynamic, the need for online and adaptive recommender systems has never been greater.

The OARS workshop has convened researchers and practitioners since 2021, and this edition brings it to the premier RecSys venue for the first time. OARS 2026 will explore the cutting edge of real-time personalization, with a special theme on agentic AI, LLM-powered recommenders, and tool-augmented systems. Specifically, we aim to:

  • Survey the landscape of online and adaptive algorithms, from reinforcement learning to in-context learning
  • Highlight emerging paradigms such as semantic ID models, agentic memory, and tool-use in recommendation
  • Address practical challenges including cold-start, tokenization, multi-modality, and distribution shift
  • Foster cross-disciplinary exchange between academia and industry

Why this workshop matters to the RecSys community

  • Static recommendation models can no longer meet user expectations for immediacy and personalization at scale
  • LLMs and foundation models are reshaping the field, yet their online adaptation and real-time integration remain open research challenges
  • Industry practitioners face unique deployment constraints that academic workshops seldom address; OARS bridges this gap
  • Predictive analytics and causal inference are increasingly used alongside RecSys, and this workshop will incorporate these adjacent areas

Call for Papers

Download CFP

OARS 2026 solicits two types of submissions:

  • Full workshop papers: up to 8 pages + references — reporting original research
  • Short/position papers: up to 4 pages + references — for position statements, preliminary results, and new ideas

All submissions undergo double-blind peer review by the Program Committee. Acceptance decisions are based on novelty, technical quality, relevance to OARS themes (especially agentic and real-time systems), and potential for community discussion.

All submissions must be formatted according to the latest ACM RecSys 2026 formatting guidelines (two column format). One recommended setting for Latex file of manuscript is: \documentclass[sigconf, anonymous, review]{acmart}. Submissions must describe work that is not previously published, not accepted for publication elsewhere, and not currently under review elsewhere. All submissions must be in English.

Please note that at least one of the authors of each accepted paper must register for the workshop and attend in person to present the paper during the workshop.

Submissions to the OARS workshop should be made at the EasyChair page.
