About the Workshop

Explore the latest advances in real-time and adaptive recommender systems — from reinforcement learning to LLM-based approaches. OARS 2026 brings together researchers and practitioners at ACM RecSys for the first time, with a special focus on agentic AI, memory-augmented recommenders, and tool-use. Running since 2021 with 60–100+ attendees per edition, OARS bridges the gap between cutting-edge research and real-world deployment.

🌟 What’s New in 2026

  • First time hosted at ACM RecSys — the premier venue for recommender systems research
  • Special theme on agentic AI, memory-augmented recommenders, and tool-use
  • Integration of LLMs with real-time recommendation
  • Expanded scope: causal inference & predictive analytics

Topics of Interest

Topics include, but are not limited to:

🤖 Agentic & LLM-based RecSys (2026 Special Theme)

  • Agentic recommender systems, assistant-style interfaces, memory and tool-use
  • LLMs and foundation models in RecSys: semantic IDs, tokenization, multi-modality, in-context learning
  • New architectures: RAG-based, streaming and event-driven, scalable learning

⚡ Online & Adaptive Learning

  • Online and continual learning, reinforcement learning, bandits, and counterfactual evaluation
  • Real-time user intent modeling, session-aware and conversational recommendation

🧩 System & Practical Challenges

  • Cold-start, distribution shift, and robustness under data sparsity
  • Industry deployments, infrastructure, and real-world case studies

📊 Evaluation, Fairness & Causal Methods

  • Evaluation, explanation, and off-policy methods for OARS
  • Predictive analytics and causal inference for recommendation
  • Privacy, ethics, fairness, and user welfare in OARS

Why Attend?

  • Hear from leading industry practitioners at Google DeepMind, Amazon, Lyft, and others on real-world deployment of adaptive recommender systems
  • Get early exposure to cutting-edge research on agentic AI, LLMs, and online learning for RecSys — the 2026 special theme
  • Network with an estimated 80–120 researchers and engineers in a focused half-day format
  • Participate in an open panel discussion: "The future of online, adaptive, and agentic recommender systems"
  • Bridge academia and industry — OARS is the premier RecSys venue with this specific focus, now brought to ACM RecSys for the first time

Call for Papers

📄 Submit Paper Download CFP

OARS 2026 solicits two types of submissions:

  • Full workshop papers: up to 8 pages + references — reporting original research
  • Short/position papers: up to 4 pages + references — for position statements, preliminary results, and new ideas

All submissions undergo double-blind peer review by the Program Committee. Acceptance decisions are based on novelty, technical quality, relevance to OARS themes (especially agentic and real-time systems), and potential for community discussion.

All submissions must be formatted according to the latest ACM RecSys 2026 formatting guidelines (two column format). One recommended setting for Latex file of manuscript is: \documentclass[sigconf, anonymous, review]{acmart}. Submissions must describe work that is not previously published, not accepted for publication elsewhere, and not currently under review elsewhere. All submissions must be in English.

Please note that at least one of the authors of each accepted paper must register for the workshop and attend in person to present the paper during the workshop.

Submissions to the OARS workshop should be made at the EasyChair page.
